## Communication

- The user is a vibe coder. Use simple, plain language in all replies, including progress updates and final answers.
- Explain what a change means for the business or the user. Use technical terms only when needed, and briefly explain unfamiliar terms and project codes.
- Keep status reports clear: what is complete, what is still pending, and the smallest next step. Distinguish planned work from implemented, tested, or production-verified work.
